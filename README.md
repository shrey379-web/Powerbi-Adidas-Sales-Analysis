# 👟 Adidas Sales Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

An interactive sales analytics dashboard built using Microsoft Power BI to analyze Adidas sales performance, profitability, product categories, regional distribution, and retailer contributions.

## 📊 Dashboard Preview

![Adidas Sales Analysis Dashboard](Adidas%20Analysis.png)

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing Adidas sales data to understand business performance across products, regions, states, and retail partners.

The dashboard transforms sales data into interactive visual insights, helping users explore revenue trends, profitability, unit sales, pricing metrics, and regional performance to support data-driven business decisions.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Monitor overall sales and profit performance.
- Analyze monthly sales trends and variations.
- Compare sales performance across regions and states.
- Identify leading product categories by sales.
- Evaluate retailer contributions to total sales.
- Present business KPIs through an interactive dashboard.

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- **Total Sales:** 899.90M
- **Total Profit:** 332.13M
- **Total Units Sold:** 2M

## 🔍 Dashboard Features

### 💰 1. Sales & Profitability Analysis

- Displays total sales and total profit.
- Tracks total units sold.
- Highlights average price per unit and average margin.
- Provides a quick overview of business performance.

### 📅 2. Monthly Sales Trend

- Visualizes sales performance across the months shown in the report.
- Highlights changes in sales volume throughout the year.
- Helps identify stronger and weaker sales periods.

### 🗺️ 3. Geographical Sales Analysis

- Uses a map to visualize sales distribution by state.
- Supports exploration of geographical sales patterns.
- Helps compare sales activity across different locations.

### 👟 4. Product Performance Analysis

- Compares sales across product categories, including men's and women's apparel and athletic footwear.
- Highlights products contributing to overall sales.
- Supports product-level performance comparisons.

### 🌎 5. Regional Sales Analysis

- Compares sales across regions, including West, Northeast, Southeast, South, and Midwest.
- Uses a pie chart to visualize regional contributions.
- Helps explore differences in regional sales performance.

### 🏪 6. Retailer Performance Analysis

- Compares sales across retailers such as West Gear, Foot Locker, Sports Direct, Kohl's, Amazon, and Walmart.
- Highlights the retailers with the largest sales contributions.
- Supports comparison of sales performance across retail partners.

### 🎛️ 7. Interactive Filters

The dashboard includes filters for:

- Region
- Price per Unit

These filters allow users to explore the report using different selection criteria.
